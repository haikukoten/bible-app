Tone: warm, clear, non-polemical. Prefer practical application over controversy.

Topics: hope in trials, prayer, forgiveness, God’s faithfulness, short reflections tied to Christian living.

Length: roughly 900–1200 words in the final article.

Language: English (US).

Avoid: political campaigning, sensationalism, or invented Bible quotes.
