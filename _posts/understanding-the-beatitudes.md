The Beatitudes are a set of teachings by Jesus that begin his Sermon on the Mount. They are found in the Gospel of Matthew, chapter 5, verses 3-12. These teachings are considered by many to be the core of Jesus' ethical and moral philosophy.

Each Beatitude begins with 'Blessed are...', which can also be translated as 'Happy are...' or 'Fortunate are...'. They describe the ideal characteristics of Jesus' followers and the rewards they will receive.

Here are the Beatitudes:

1. Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.
2. Blessed are those who mourn, for they will be comforted.
3. Blessed are the meek, for they will inherit the earth.
4. Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, for they will be filled.
5. Blessed are the merciful, for they will be shown mercy.
6. Blessed are the pure in heart, for they will see God.
7. Blessed are the peacemakers, for they will be called children of God.
8. Blessed are those who are persecuted because of righteousness, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.

These teachings encourage humility, compassion, righteousness, and peace. They promise spiritual rewards for those who embody these qualities, even in the face of worldly challenges or persecution.

Understanding and living out the Beatitudes is a lifelong journey for many Christians, as they seek to align their lives with Jesus' teachings and grow in their faith.
